Privacy Back

Scan runs in this browser. The job text is not sent to ApplySafe and not sent to a model.

A job-page scan can be saved on this device: the time, the site, the page title, the verdict, and the score. The posting text is not saved. Clear that list from the dashboard.

Do not rely on this extension to store a social insurance number or a passport photo. It reads the words on the page to find a request. It does not need the number.
